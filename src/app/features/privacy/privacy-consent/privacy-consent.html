Your privacy at CareBridge

To ask a question, we need your permission to use your data. We save your name, your email address and your answers, so your GP practice can review your question and put the most urgent questions first.

Your data is safe

  • Only you and the staff of your practice can read your questions.
  • Your answers, name and email address are stored encrypted.
  • Your password is never stored, only a hash of it.
  • Everything you send travels over a secure connection.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

You can change your mind at any time on the privacy page, which also explains everything in full.
