Privacy at CareBridge

Version of 11 November 2026

CareBridge is a student project. Please do not enter real medical information.

What we store

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your answers: the area of your complaint, how long you have had it, how much it bothers you, your symptoms, medication, treatment and your description.
  • When you sent your question, and when you gave permission.
  • If you use email to sign in: whether you confirmed your email address, and a hash of the confirmation code.
  • If you use email to sign in: a hash of your password, never the password itself.
  • Technical logs with IP addresses, to find faults. When you create an account, a hash of your IP address is kept for one hour, to stop accounts being created in bulk.

Why we need it

Your practice needs your answers to review your question, and your name and email address to know who is asking. We use your answers to calculate a score that puts the most urgent questions first. Staff can always change that order, and a person reviews every question.

Health data needs your explicit permission, so we ask for it before your first question.

Who can see it

  • You see your own questions.
  • The GP and the GP assistants of the practice see all questions.
  • Microsoft Azure hosts CareBridge in Sweden.
  • An email service sends the code that confirms your email address. It receives only your email address and the code, never your questions.
  • Google handles the sign-in if you choose it. The sign-in page loads a script from Google, so Google can see your IP address, even if you use your email address.

We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

How your data is protected

  • Your answers, name and email address are stored encrypted, so a copy of the database does not show them.
  • Your account ID and the email address used for signing in are stored as a hash.
  • Passwords are stored as a BCrypt hash, and an account locks after too many wrong passwords.
  • Every connection is encrypted, and every question needs a signed-in user with the right role.

How long we keep it

Questions and accounts are not deleted automatically yet. Logs are kept for 30 days and backups for 7 days.

Your rights

You can see your questions under My questions. You also have the right to a copy of your data, and to have it corrected or deleted. These are not in the app yet, so please ask your practice. You can withdraw your permission at any time; what you already sent stays stored until it is deleted.
